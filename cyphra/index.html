A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Before you make yourself at home.

Is Cyphra free?

Yes. Cyphra is free to use, and core browsing does not require an account. Local AI runs with Ollama on your own hardware. The macOS installer downloads and verifies the runtime and starter model for you.

Does local AI mean everything works offline?

Local model inference can run on your computer once Ollama and a model are installed. Websites, live news, searches and initial model downloads still require a network connection.

Can I navigate all four panes independently?

Yes. Each pane can display and navigate a different page, and existing tabs can be arranged into a split layout. Panes share the browser profile, so they are not isolated login sessions.

How do I talk to Cyphra?

Enable voice in the browser and use the wake word “Cyphra,” then say a command such as “navigate to google.com.” Voice availability depends on your platform, microphone permissions and installed speech model. The Windows and macOS voice paths are verified implementations.

Are macOS and Linux installers available?

Verified Windows and macOS installers are available now. The Linux package remains in preparation.

Is the Research Agent available today?

Not yet. Autonomous multi-site research is in development and is not included in the current release. The website shows a concept preview of the planned experience.
