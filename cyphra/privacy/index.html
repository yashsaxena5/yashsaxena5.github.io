Your privacy.
The details.

This notice describes the launch website and explains the boundaries of Cyphra’s local AI features.

This website

The site serves its own fonts, scripts and visuals. It does not include analytics, advertising trackers or third-party embeds, and does not set tracking cookies. Interactive product previews run in your browser.

Email preview

The research notification form is a preview. It does not transmit, register or persist an email address. A clear notice will be provided before an actual subscription service is introduced.

Website hosting and downloads

The service hosting this website and the service distributing installers may receive standard request information such as your IP address and browser details. Their infrastructure may keep access or security logs. This website does not add its own visitor tracking.

Local AI in the desktop browser

When Cyphra uses a local Ollama model, inference takes place on your computer. Local mode does not require sending prompts or page context to a cloud AI provider. Ordinary browsing, website searches, news feeds and downloading models still contact external services.

Saved credentials

The desktop credential vault stores saved logins locally. Using a saved login on a website still sends it to that website as part of signing in. The four browsing panes share the browser profile; they are not separate privacy containers.

Permission and control

Voice features need microphone access. Consequential browser actions use confirmation, and sensitive profile filling requires a visible approval. Feature availability can differ by platform.

Future services

If cloud AI, accounts, telemetry or an email subscription service are introduced, this notice and the relevant controls must be updated before those services are activated.
