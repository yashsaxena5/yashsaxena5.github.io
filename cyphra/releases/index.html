Cyphra for Windows 1.5.0

A new, calmer look on a much newer, safer engine.

  • Chromium 140 engine with security fixes up to Chromium 154 (was Chromium 118).
  • No tab strip or toolbar: an oval address bar on top, glass tools that appear at the right edge, 11 themes.
  • Warnings for known phishing and malware sites, checked on your PC; https first; certificate errors refused.
  • Risky downloads wait for your OK and are scanned by Windows Defender.
  • The local copilot can read the page you ask about; sign-ins survive a restart.
