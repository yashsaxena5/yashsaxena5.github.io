Cyphra for Windows 1.6.4

Every AI feature now runs on Gemma 4 26B-A4B, about five times faster on an ordinary laptop than 1.6.2-1.6.3.

  • One local model for the copilot, deep page analysis (it reads screenshots), summaries, voice and study material: Gemma 4 26B-A4B (about 15 GB). Only about 4B of its 26B parameters work per word, so it is quick on a CPU.
  • Measured on a 32 GB laptop, CPU only: about 76 tokens/s reading and 15 tokens/s writing, against 14 and 3 for Qwen 3.8. A full page analysis of a long article took about 8 minutes instead of an hour or more.
  • The installer’s AI step downloads only Gemma 4; 32 GB of RAM is recommended.
