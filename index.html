My story

From an amphibious quadcopter to cryogenic silicon

2015 – 2019Sultanpur, India

Learning by building

I earned my B.Tech in Electrical Engineering at Kamla Nehru Institute of Technology, Sultanpur, and spent most of those years building things. My biggest project was ACUSPED, the Amphibious Copter, Underwater Submersible Propelling Engineered Device: a quadcopter that could fly through the air and float on water. The college and the Government of Uttar Pradesh funded the whole project.

I also founded the institute's Entrepreneurship Cell. In 2017 it secured ₹25,00,000 (₹25 lakh) in funding, and we went on to organise two international conferences.

2019 – 2021Kathmandu, Nepal

My first FPGA job

As an FPGA Engineer at Hospo Tech Nepal (Hotel Yak & Yeti), I built real-time FPGA control systems for autonomous solar tracking. I designed a custom system-on-programmable-chip on an Altera Cyclone II, with a Nios II/f soft-core processor on the Avalon-MM fabric. I also wrote Verilog and VHDL IP cores for fuzzy-logic control and stepper-motor sequencing. Moving that maths from software into hardware cut control-loop latency by 40%.

2021 – 2023Chicago, Illinois

Graduate school in the U.S.

I moved to the United States for an M.S. in Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Illinois Chicago. In Prof. Sudip K. Mazumder's LESES lab I worked on research papers and hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) systems. I also served as a teaching assistant, and a scholarship fully funded my degree. In my second year I worked with Prof. Amit Trivedi. I graduated in May 2023 with a 3.75 GPA.

2023 – todayBatavia, Illinois

Chips for physics and quantum computing

In July 2023 I joined Fermilab, America's particle physics and accelerator laboratory, as an ASIC Engineer. I work across the whole life of a chip, from RTL and verification through FPGA firmware to bring-up inside a cryostat. Our collaborators include NASA, Caltech, MIT, CERN and Microsoft. In 2025 I received Fermilab's Technical Excellence Award for cutting three months off Quantinuum testing.

Projects

Things I've built

At Fermilab

SUNROCK

32-channel cryogenic readout ASIC

Readout RTL in SystemVerilog with fine-delay TDCs and bubble-error filtering, multi-channel priority arbiters and high-speed CML serializers. Configuration registers use triple modular redundancy with live SEU counters. Slow control runs over a custom GPPI link and an I²C-to-Wishbone bridge. I verified it in UVM with Fermilab's Songbird framework, then brought it up on a ZCU102 (Caribou) board with PetaLinux and AXI firmware, matching silicon against Cocotb and Xcelium runs.

Cryogenic PLL bring-up

Debug and characterization at 4 K

The PLL would not lock on silicon at 4 K. I worked through VCO and loop analysis, retuned the VCO sub-bands and loop parameters, and restored stable lock with 1.875 ps RMS jitter, which brought the chip back to full function.

Verified the chip for tape-out readiness, then validated it on the bench down to 77 K and matched the silicon results to simulation, which reduced surprises after silicon came back.
